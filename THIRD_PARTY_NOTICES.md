# Third-Party Dictionary Data

The optional dictionary lookup and generated local dictionary use data from:

- [ECDICT](https://github.com/skywind3000/ECDICT), a free English-Chinese dictionary database under the MIT License.
- [FreeDictionaryAPI.com](https://freedictionaryapi.com/), sourced from Wiktionary under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
- [Wiktionary](https://en.wiktionary.org/) under CC BY-SA 4.0.
- [MyMemory](https://mymemory.translated.net/) for Chinese translation references.

The website keeps its authored HMI glossary entries separate and gives them priority over third-party dictionary results.
